About CobraTrust

CobraTrust is a business trust-profile platform created to help businesses present themselves professionally online and give potential customers useful information before they decide to contact or do business with them.

Businesses today often communicate with customers through social media, messaging apps, websites and other online channels. However, customers may still have difficulty determining who they are dealing with, what the business does, what work it has completed and what information they can rely on.

CobraTrust brings important business information together into one professional public profile.

Our goal is straightforward: help businesses communicate trust more clearly before business begins.

How CobraTrust works

CobraTrust is designed around a simple journey: create your profile, build trust, share it and understand how customers interact with it.

1. Create your account

A business creates a CobraTrust account and provides the information needed to build its business profile.

2. Complete your business profile

Businesses can add information such as their business name, description, contact details, social links, portfolio and relevant trust proof.

3. Build your public trust profile

CobraTrust organizes the information into a professional public profile that customers can access through a shareable profile link.

4. Customers review the profile

Visitors can learn about the business, review its available trust proof and portfolio material and access the business's available contact options.

5. Businesses understand their audience

CobraTrust analytics can record profile views and interactions such as contact clicks, trust-proof activity, portfolio activity and other supported events.

Important: CobraTrust is a trust-information platform. A verified profile is not a guarantee that a business, product, service or transaction is completely risk-free.

CobraTrust public trust profiles

A CobraTrust public profile gives a business a single professional place to present itself to potential customers.

Trust proof

Portfolio

Businesses are responsible for making sure that information they publish on their public profile is accurate, lawful and appropriate for public viewing.

Verification

CobraTrust verification is designed to provide an additional trust signal by reviewing information submitted through the platform's verification process.

What verification means

When a business receives a CobraTrust verification status, it means the submitted information has been reviewed according to the verification process applicable to that profile or service.

What verification does not mean

  • It does not guarantee that every transaction with the business will be successful.
  • It does not guarantee the quality of every product or service.
  • It does not guarantee future business conduct.
  • It does not replace customer due diligence.
  • It does not mean CobraTrust is a party to a transaction between a business and its customer.

Maintaining verification

CobraTrust may review or request additional information when necessary. Verification may be suspended or removed if information is found to be inaccurate, misleading or inconsistent with the platform's requirements.

Payment verification

Where manual payment verification is used, users may be required to provide payment information and upload a payment screenshot.

The submitted payment information is reviewed by the CobraTrust Verification Team. Submitting a form does not automatically mean that a payment has been approved.

Payment approval

A subscription that depends on manual approval becomes active only after the payment has been reviewed and approved through the CobraTrust payment-verification process.

Payment safety: Always follow the official payment instructions displayed inside CobraTrust. Do not send money to payment details obtained from unofficial messages or third parties.

Security and data protection

CobraTrust is designed with account security, controlled access and protected data handling in mind.

Account authentication

CobraTrust may use Firebase Authentication or other authentication infrastructure to securely manage user accounts and sign-in.

Database security

Information required to operate the platform may be stored using secure cloud infrastructure. Access to private account information should be restricted according to the platform's security rules.

Payment information

Payment-verification submissions are intended to be accessible only to authorized personnel responsible for reviewing payments.

Anonymous visitors

Public profile visitors may be represented by anonymous identifiers or sessions for analytics purposes without requiring the visitor to create a CobraTrust account.

No internet platform can promise absolute security. Users should avoid sharing passwords, authentication codes or unnecessary sensitive information with anyone.

Frequently asked questions

What is CobraTrust?
CobraTrust is a platform that helps businesses create professional public trust profiles containing business information, trust proof, portfolio material and contact options.
Do visitors need a CobraTrust account to view a public profile?
No. Public CobraTrust profiles can be viewed by people who do not have a CobraTrust account, subject to the profile's subscription and availability status.
What does the CobraTrust verified status mean?
It indicates that the business or submitted information has passed the applicable CobraTrust verification process. It does not guarantee future business conduct or a successful transaction.
Can a business see profile activity?
CobraTrust provides analytics for supported profile activity, including profile views and interactions. Some visitors may be recorded anonymously rather than by name.
Can someone view a profile without signing up?
Yes. Public profile visitors do not necessarily need to create a CobraTrust account.
What happens when a subscription expires?
Features that require an active subscription may become unavailable until the business renews its subscription.
Does submitting payment automatically activate my profile?
No. Where manual payment verification is used, the payment must be reviewed and approved before dependent features become active.
Does CobraTrust guarantee a business?
No. CobraTrust provides trust and business-presentation tools. Customers should still perform their own appropriate checks before sending money or entering into an agreement.
How can I get help?
Use the official support option provided within the CobraTrust platform.

Need help?

If you need assistance with your account, public trust profile, verification, analytics, subscription or payment verification, use the official CobraTrust support channel.
