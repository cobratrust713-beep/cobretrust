The missing layer of trust in online commerce.

Selling online is harder when buyers can't verify who you are. We built CobraTrust to bridge the gap between independent sellers and cautious buyers.

The Problem

Buyers hesitate when dealing with independent sellers. They worry about scams, undelivered goods, or misrepresented services. When you only have a social media handle or an email address, establishing credibility takes time you don't have.

  • Lost sales due to buyer hesitation
  • Time wasted proving your legitimacy
  • Scattered reviews across different platforms
  • Unprofessional first impressions

The Solution

TrustCobra provides a verified, professional presence. One shareable link that instantly communicates your credibility, centralizes your proof of work, and gives buyers the confidence they need to proceed with payment.

  • Instant professional credibility
  • Centralized reviews and portfolio
  • Clear contact and verification status
  • Higher conversion from inquiry to payment
